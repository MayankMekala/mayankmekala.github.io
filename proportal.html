Wonder Dynamics

Sci-Fi Short · Wonder Studio
Baton · Short Film AI-Driven VFX Wonder Studio
Overview

Baton is a narrative sci-fi short film produced to demonstrate Wonder Dynamics' Wonder Studio — an AI-driven toolset that automates character and VFX integration. The film uses a grounded, street-level story about responsibility and climate futures to show how Wonder Studio can streamline complex compositing work while retaining a cinematic, emotionally-driven look.

  • DisciplineNarrative short film · VFX integration
  • RoleWriter / Director / Editor / Colorist
  • ContextPromotional piece highlighting Wonder Dynamics technology
Production
Camera: iPhone 15 Pro Max.
Shot as a fast, location-based production with handheld and gimbal work to keep the imagery energetic and grounded while leaving space for seamless CG character integration via Wonder Studio.
Post-Production
Characters and VFX passes were generated and composited using Wonder Studio, with edit, sound, and finishing completed in DaVinci Resolve and Adobe After Effects.
Wonder Studio DaVinci Resolve Adobe After Effects

Page Mill Winery

Video & Motion Graphics · Winery
Video & Motion Graphics Brand Film
Overview

Promotional brand film and logo-driven motion graphic package for Page Mill Winery, combining cinematic vineyard imagery with a clean, animated logo lockup. The logo motion is woven into the hero film and supporting video deliverables, keeping the winery's identity present from title card through end frame.

  • DisciplineVideo & Motion Graphics
  • ScopeHero brand film · Logo animation · Social cutdowns
  • ContextPromotional content for web, tasting room, and social channels
Production
On-location shoot across vineyard, cellar, and tasting room spaces, focusing on natural light, texture, and small details that communicate the craft of the winery.
Post-Production
Edited and graded in DaVinci Resolve, with a warm, filmic grade tailored to late-afternoon vineyard tones. Logo motion and titles were built to feel subtle and premium, syncing with the pacing and music of the brand film.
DaVinci Resolve Adobe After Effects
