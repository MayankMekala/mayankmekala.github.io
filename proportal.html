Architect Bio

Mayank Mekala / MKX

Operator · Creative Director · Filmmaker · Editor & Colorist · Photographer · Graphic & Brand Designer · Game Developer · Web & Interface Designer

From the mind of Mayank Mekala (MKX), the MKXSPHERE is a self-contained, single-author studio and online nexus for multi-format work: films, games, visual systems, and digital interfaces. Every component of the framework — from core site architecture to archived projects — is authored, designed, and operated by Mekala as the sole Architect and Operator.

Based in the San Francisco Bay Area, Mekala has been building images and stories since high school: shooting action shorts, practicing wildlife photography, and designing cover art for emerging artists in the Bay Area underground rap scene. He later studied film and psychology at the University of California, Davis, where his circle of collaborators coined the moniker MKX for his ambitious, against-the-grain approach.

What began as a personal brand evolved into the MKXSPHERE in 2025 — an ecosystem of focused "divisions" for specific mediums:

· Film work presented in a tightly curated, streaming-style catalog
· Games framed like an early-2000s console menu
· Graphic design and photography organized as a precision-built digital zine

As MKX grows, the MKXSPHERE grows with it — expanding into film, game development, photography, graphic design, web design, and beyond. While it is rooted in a single-operator model, the platform also provides space for collaborators and artists whose work aligns with the MKX ethos.

Professionally, Mekala is a creative and product-minded Video Producer with 7+ years of experience overseeing end-to-end production across diverse locations, crews, and formats. He manages projects from concept through final delivery — keeping work on-strategy, on-budget, and at a high bar of craft — and often functions as a liaison between Creative, Brand, and Production teams. He is technically fluent in Adobe Creative Suite, DaVinci Resolve, motion systems, and front-end development (HTML/CSS/JS) for digital marketing and interactive experiences.

As a Video Editor and Colorist, Mekala brings a detailed, systems-driven approach to visual storytelling, motion graphics, and brand-forward content. When he is not in production, he is deep in niche corners of the internet, studying other artists' work not as passive consumption, but as source material to be metabolized, reframed, and re-deployed inside the MKX system.

The MKXSPHERE is now at the crux of its emergence — a young, evolving ecosystem seeking new collaborators, new mediums, and new ways of experiencing artwork for a generation fluent in both the emerging technologies and traditional craftsmanship.
